Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The short version: Your recordings are stored on your device. They only leave it if you choose to: transcribing a recording, using Polish, title suggestions or summaries (these use cloud AI services, and the App asks your permission first), backing recordings up to the cloud, or sharing one. You can delete your cloud data, or your whole account, at any time from within the App.

This Privacy Policy explains how Backflipped Studios LLC ("Backflipped Studios," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Talk About The Day mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

Account information

When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive basic account information such as your name, email address, and a unique user identifier. This is handled through Firebase Authentication (a Google service) and is used to secure your account and sync your data across devices.

Content you create

The App is built around audio recordings and their transcripts. Recordings you make are stored locally on your device. Transcripts are made in the cloud, and only when you ask for one or turn on automatic transcription. If you transcribe a recording without cloud backup, its audio is uploaded only to be transcribed and is deleted from our storage afterward.

If you back up your recordings, your audio, transcripts, titles, dates, and (if you turned on location tagging) recording locations are uploaded to your private storage area in Firebase (Google Cloud) so you can restore them or access them on another device. Cloud backup is optional and under your control.

Cloud AI features

Transcription and the other AI features use third-party AI services. The App explains this and asks for your permission before any of them is used for the first time:

  • Transcription sends a recording's audio to OpenAI to produce a transcript. If OpenAI is unavailable, the audio is sent to Google Cloud Speech-to-Text instead.
  • Polish, title suggestions and summaries send transcript text to OpenAI.

These services process the data only to return the result to you. Under their API terms, neither uses it to train their models. If you don't use these features, nothing is sent to them. You can withdraw this permission at any time in Settings → Cloud AI permission; the App will ask again before it next uses one of these features. Signed-in users without Premium get a few free transcriptions; we keep a count of how many you've used.

Sharing

When you share a recording, the App creates a private link that expires (after 24 hours by default). Anyone you give the link to can listen to that recording until it expires.

Device permissions

  • Microphone — required to record audio. Recording only happens when you actively start a recording.
  • Location — if you grant permission, an approximate or precise location may be attached to a recording so you can remember where a memory took place. This is optional and off by default; the App works without it. Locations are saved with your recordings and included in your cloud backup if you use it.
  • Notifications — used to remind you and to let you know when a recording has finished transcribing.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. We do not receive or store your credit card or payment details — those are handled entirely by the app store. We receive information such as which subscription you hold and whether it is active.

Usage and diagnostic data

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the App is used and to diagnose crashes and errors. This may include device type, operating system version, app version, general usage events, and crash reports. This information helps us improve stability and features.

How we use your information

  • To provide the App's core functionality — recording, transcription, optional cloud backup, and sharing.
  • To authenticate you and sync your content across your devices.
  • To manage subscriptions and premium features.
  • To send notifications you have enabled.
  • To diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and improve the App.

How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate the App:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check) — account, synced content, and diagnostics.
  • Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In — authentication.
  • OpenAI — transcription (audio), and Polish, title suggestions and summaries (transcript text).
  • Google Cloud Speech-to-Text — transcription (audio) when OpenAI is unavailable.
  • OpenStreetMap — map images for the memory map. Your device downloads them directly, which shares your IP address with OpenStreetMap's servers, but not your recordings.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or Backflipped Studios.

Data retention and deletion

Recordings stored locally remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Content you back up to the cloud is retained until you delete it. You can delete all of your cloud backups from the App's Settings. You can also permanently delete your account in Settings → Delete account. This removes your account, cloud recordings, transcripts, and subscription records from our systems. You can also request deletion by email (see Delete your account). Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; manage that in your store account settings.

Security

We use industry-standard measures, including the security infrastructure provided by Google Firebase, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

The App is meant to be used by adults, such as parents. If you record your child, that recording is your content: it stays on your device unless you choose to back it up, transcribe it or share it, and you can delete it at any time, both from your device and from the cloud.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may also be communicated within the App.
