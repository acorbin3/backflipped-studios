Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

The short version: Your audio recordings are transcribed on your device — we never send your audio to a third party to transcribe it. We only store your recordings in the cloud if you choose to back them up, and you can delete that cloud data at any time from within the app.

This Privacy Policy explains how Backflipped Studios LLC ("Backflipped Studios," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Talk About The Day mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

Account information

When you sign in — including with Google Sign-In — we receive basic account information such as your name, email address, and a unique user identifier. This is handled through Firebase Authentication (a Google service) and is used to secure your account and sync your data across devices.

Content you create

The App is built around audio recordings and their transcripts. Recordings you make are stored locally on your device. Transcription is performed on-device using an embedded speech-recognition model — your audio is not uploaded to us or any third party for transcription.

If you enable cloud backup, your recordings and associated transcripts are uploaded to your private storage area in Firebase (Google Cloud) so you can restore them or access them on another device. Cloud backup is optional and under your control.

Device permissions

  • Microphone — required to record audio. Recording only happens when you actively start a recording.
  • Location — if you grant permission, an approximate or precise location may be attached to a recording so you can remember where a memory took place. This is optional; the App works without it, and location data stays associated with your own recordings.
  • Notifications — used to remind you and to let you know when a recording has finished transcribing.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. We do not receive or store your credit card or payment details — those are handled entirely by the app store. We receive information such as which subscription you hold and whether it is active.

Usage and diagnostic data

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the App is used and to diagnose crashes and errors. This may include device type, operating system version, app version, general usage events, and crash reports. This information helps us improve stability and features.

How we use your information

  • To provide the App's core functionality — recording, on-device transcription, and optional cloud backup.
  • To authenticate you and sync your content across your devices.
  • To manage subscriptions and premium features.
  • To send notifications you have enabled.
  • To diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and improve the App.

How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate the App:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check) — account, synced content, and diagnostics.
  • Google Sign-In — authentication.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or Backflipped Studios.

Data retention and deletion

Recordings stored locally remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Content you back up to the cloud is retained until you delete it. The App includes a feature to delete all of your cloud backups, and you may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us.

Security

We use industry-standard measures, including the security infrastructure provided by Google Firebase, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may also be communicated within the App.
