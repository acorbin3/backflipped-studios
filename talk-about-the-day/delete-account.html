Delete your account

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Delete it in the App

Open Talk About The Day, go to Settings, and tap Delete account. Your account and data are deleted right away.

Or ask us by email

If you no longer have the App installed, email backflippedstudios@gmail.com with the subject Delete My Account, from or including the email address you signed in with. We'll confirm and complete the deletion within 7 business days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (name, email address, and user ID)
  • All recordings, transcripts, titles, and locations backed up to the cloud
  • Your subscription records in our subscription service (RevenueCat)

We don't keep any of this data after deletion. Recordings saved only on your phone are not stored by us. They stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the App.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
