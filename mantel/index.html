Help & support

We usually reply within two business days.

Contact us: hello@backflippedstudios.com
Include the email you sign in with and your phone model if something isn't working.

Where are my projects stored?

On the free plan, and when you use the app as a guest, everything — photos and details — is saved on your phone only. With cloud backup, it's also saved securely in the cloud so you can restore it on a new phone or see it on the web.

I got a new phone. How do I get my projects back?

If you have cloud backup, install the app and sign in with the same account — your projects download automatically. Free-plan projects can't be moved, because they only ever existed on the old phone.

I'm a guest. Can I keep my projects?

Yes. Open the menu → Account & privacy → Create account or sign in. Everything on the phone moves over to your account.

How do I restore my subscription?

Sign in with the same account, open the menu → Upgrade to premium, and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from:

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: menu → Account & privacy → Delete account. Or see delete your account.
