Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Backflipped Studios LLC ("we", "us") handles information in Mantel ("the app"). Mantel helps parents save photos of and notes about their children's projects. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have. Short version: on the free plan everything stays on your phone; we never sell your data and the app shows no ads.

Who the app is for

Mantel is made for parents and other adults. It is not directed to children, and children should not create accounts. Information about a child (such as a first name, birthday or school year) is entered by the parent, who stays in control of it and can change or delete it at any time.

What we collect and where it lives

InformationWhyWhere it is stored
Your projects: photos, titles, descriptions, tags, dates; kid profiles: name, optional birthday, age and school year This is the app's core purpose Free plan and guests: only on your device. We never receive it.
Cloud backup (paid): also in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage) so you can restore it and see it on other devices.
Account details: email address, and the name Apple or Google share if you sign in with them; a user ID. Guests get an anonymous ID only. To sign you in and tie a subscription to you Google Firebase Authentication
Subscription status: which plan you have, when it renews or expires, and your user ID To unlock cloud backup and restore purchases RevenueCat (our subscription service) and Google Firebase. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google — we never see your card details.
Usage analytics: which screens and features are used, counts such as how many projects you have (in ranges), device model, OS and app version, an app-instance identifier To understand what works and improve the app Google Analytics for Firebase. It never includes your photos, project text or your kids' names.
Crash reports: stack traces, device state at the time of a crash, your user ID To find and fix bugs Firebase Crashlytics
App integrity checks: a token from Apple App Attest / DeviceCheck or Google Play Integrity To stop other software abusing our servers Firebase App Check

The app asks for camera and photo-library access only to take or pick the project photos you choose to save. It does not access your location, contacts or microphone, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We use these service providers to run the app, each bound by its own privacy and security terms: Google Firebase, RevenueCat, and Apple or Google for payments. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Security

Data sent to the cloud is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access rules ensure each account can read only its own data. Free-plan data on your device is protected by your device's own security.

How long we keep it

Cloud-backup data is kept while your account exists. If a subscription ends, data already backed up is kept so it's there if you resubscribe; copies already on your phone stay on your phone. You can delete it any time by deleting your account. When you delete your account, your cloud data, your account and your subscription record are deleted immediately; residual copies in service-provider logs and backups expire within 30 days. Analytics data is retained for no more than 14 months.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete any project, photo or kid profile in the app at any time.
  • Delete your account and all its data in the app: menu → Account & privacy → Delete account. You can also request deletion on the web.
  • Access or correct your information, or ask a question, by emailing us (below). Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have additional rights; we honor them for all users.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live). If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for material changes we will let you know in the app.
