Delete your account

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Mantel and sign in.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu on the home screen.
  3. Choose Account & privacy, then Delete account, and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email (no app needed)

Email hello@backflippedstudios.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject Delete my Mantel account. We'll confirm the request and delete the account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email and user ID).
  • All cloud-backup data: every project, photo, description and kid profile.
  • Your subscription record at our subscription provider.
  • Data the app saved on the phone you delete from.

Residual copies in our service providers' logs and backups expire within 30 days. Anonymous analytics and crash statistics that can no longer be linked to you may be kept for up to 14 months.

Have a subscription? Deleting your account does not cancel it — Apple or Google keeps billing until you cancel. Cancel first in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
