Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The short version: Crypto-TA has no accounts and no ads. Your settings, favourites and price alerts stay on your device. The App sends us usage statistics and crash reports that are not tied to your name or an account.

This Privacy Policy explains how Backflipped Studios LLC ("Backflipped Studios," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Crypto-TA mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

The App has no sign-in and no advertising, and it does not use an advertising identifier. We never receive your name, email address, contacts or precise location through the App.

The App uses two Google Firebase services to tell us how it is used and when it fails:

  • Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics). Which screens are opened and which features are used, for example the coin, time period or indicator you pick, or that an alert was set. The alert price itself is not sent. Each installation is given a random identifier so that events from one installation can be counted together.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). When the App crashes or hits an error: what went wrong, where in the code, and the device model, operating system version and App version.

Google processes this data for us and, like any internet service, sees your device's IP address when it is sent. It is handled under Google's privacy policy. We use it only to find and fix problems and to decide what to improve. We do not use it for advertising, we do not combine it with other data to identify you, and we do not sell it.

Turning this off

You can stop the App sending usage statistics and crash reports at any time. Open the menu (the three dots on the chart screen) and untick Share usage statistics. Nothing more is sent after that, and crash reports still waiting on your device are deleted. Tick it again to turn it back on. If you do not see this option, update the App to the latest version.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • Usage statistics that carry an installation identifier are kept for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically. After that only totals remain, such as how many times a feature was used.
  • Crash reports are kept for 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Feedback emails are kept only as long as we need them to reply and follow up.

We hold nothing else about you. There is no account, so there is no profile or history on our servers.

Usage statistics and crash reports carry a random installation identifier and not your name, so we cannot look them up from your name or email address. Uninstalling the App ends the identifier, and a fresh install gets a new one. To have a feedback email deleted, or to ask anything else about your data, write to hello@backflippedstudios.com.

Information stored on your device

The App keeps the following on your device only. It is never sent to us:

  • The coin, currency and time period you last viewed.
  • Your indicator choices, their settings and line colours.
  • Your favourite coins and price alerts.
  • Cached price and market data, so charts load faster.

Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data, removes all of it.

Market data providers

To show prices and charts, the App requests public market data directly from Coinbase and CoinGecko. These requests contain the coin and time period being requested. As with any internet request, those providers can see your device's IP address and basic request details, and they handle that information under their own privacy policies. We do not receive it.

Notifications

If you set a price alert, the App asks for permission to show notifications. Alerts are checked on your device and the notification is created on your device. No push-notification service or account is involved.

Sharing and feedback

If you share a chart, the App hands the image to the app you choose (for example, messages or email). If you send feedback, the App opens your own email app, and we receive only what you choose to send. We use that email solely to reply to you.

How your information is shared

We do not sell your information. The usage statistics and crash reports described above are processed by Google on our behalf, and we do not share them with anyone else.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.
