Color by Numbers

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

The short version: Color by Numbers collects nothing about your child or you. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no purchases. Coloring progress stays on the device. The app only goes online to download new pictures.

This Privacy Policy explains how Backflipped Studios LLC ("Backflipped Studios," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you or your child use the Color by Numbers mobile application (the "App"). The App is a coloring app for young children and is designed to collect nothing about your child or you.

What we collect

Nothing. The App does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The App never asks for a name, age, birthday, email address, phone number or photo.
  • The App does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo library.
  • The App does not create or read advertising identifiers or any other device identifier.

No ads, tracking or purchases

  • The App shows no advertising.
  • The App contains no analytics, tracking, crash-reporting or social-media software from us or from any third party.
  • The App has no in-app purchases.
  • The App shows children no links to websites, app stores or other apps.

What stays on the device

Coloring progress (which shapes have been colored) and settings (sound, theme, easy mode) are saved only on the device. This information never leaves the device and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you remove the App, or you can clear progress in Settings under Reset All Progress.

Internet use

The App goes online for one purpose: downloading new pictures to color from our picture catalog at color-by-numbers-art.web.app, which is hosted on Firebase Hosting, a Google service.

  • These are ordinary file downloads. The App sends no information about your child, no identifiers and nothing about how the App is used.
  • As with any internet request, the hosting provider's servers receive the device's IP address in order to deliver the files, and may keep standard server logs. We do not use those logs to identify or profile anyone. Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • The App works without an internet connection using the pictures already on the device.

Grown-ups area

Settings are behind a question for grown-ups so that children cannot change them or reset progress by accident.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for children and is built to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Apple App Store rules for the Kids category and the Google Play Families policy. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for a parent to review, correct or delete, and no parental consent is requested.

Changes to this policy

If the App ever changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this policy and its "Last updated" date before the change is released. The current policy is also available inside the App under Settings.
