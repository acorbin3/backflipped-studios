Color by Numbers

A coloring app for kids aged 2 to 5. Pick a paint pot, tap the shapes with the same number, and watch the picture come to life — no reading needed, no ads, and nothing collected about your child.

For grown-ups

Opening settings. Tap the small cog on the home screen and answer the multiplication question. Settings hold sound, themes, easy mode and resetting progress.

Easy mode. For the youngest players: any tap colors a shape with its right color, with no number matching needed.

Zooming. Pinch the picture with two fingers to zoom in on small shapes. One finger always colors.

New pictures. The app downloads new pictures when it is online, and keeps working offline with the ones it already has.
